# DSLRBooth vs. LumaBooth vs. Easy Picsy: Which Photobooth Software Should You Use?

If you're starting or running a photobooth business, the most important decision you'll make isn't just about cameras or printers—it's about **software**.

Your software is the brain of your booth. It manages the photos, designs, printing, sharing, and sometimes even payments. Get it right, and your business runs smoothly. Get it wrong, and you'll be stuck with frustrated guests, complicated setups, and missed opportunities.

In this guide, we'll compare three big names: **DSLRBooth, LumaBooth, and Easy Picsy**—so you can decide which one works best for your business.

---

## DSLRBooth Overview

**What it is:** DSLRBooth is one of the most established photobooth apps, known for its professional DSLR camera integrations.

**Pros:**

- Works well with DSLR cameras for high-quality shots
- Advanced editing tools and templates
- Good for experienced booth owners

**Cons:**

- Can be complicated for beginners
- License-based pricing (can get expensive)
- Doesn't support PH-specific payments like GCash or QRPH

💡 *Best for:* Experienced operators who want full DSLR integration and don't mind a steeper learning curve.

---

## LumaBooth Overview

**What it is:** LumaBooth is another popular app, mainly geared toward iPad-based photobooths.

**Pros:**

- Affordable monthly pricing
- Easy to set up with iPads
- Cloud storage and instant sharing

**Cons:**

- Limited DSLR support
- Fewer customization options compared to DSLRBooth
- No local payment integrations (GCash/QRPH)

💡 *Best for:* Booth owners who want a budget-friendly iPad solution with decent sharing features.
